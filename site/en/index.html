Put a roster of agents on delivery

Most tools ask you to write instruction after instruction. In Legion you act as the general: you state the goal, the roster breaks it into a staged task chain and works through it — while the diffs, test evidence and acceptance records settle back onto the task. Your code, terminal and credentials never leave your own machine.

01 — Capabilities

You issue goals, not chores

Most tools ask you to write instruction after instruction. Legion asks what you want done. The roster splits it, assigns it, executes and verifies it — and leaves the evidence on the task. You just accept the result.

01

A goal becomes a task chain

Publish one goal; it is split into staged tasks that flow by dependency. Every transition runs through a state machine — traceable, pausable, cancellable.

02

A roster, each with one job

Every space has its own roster. The software pipeline ships with eight roles, from requirements clarification all the way to deploy and CI/CD.

03

Code stays on your machine

Local-first: repos, terminal, build tools and model credentials live on your computer. The Hub only handles identity, routing and records.

04

Evidence settles onto the task

Patches, diffs, test output, artifacts and acceptance records all hang off the same task. Acceptance never leaves the browser.

02 — Architecture

Three layers, each doing its part

The Hub runs neither your code nor a large model. Your computer dials out from inside the network, so no inbound port has to be opened on the router.

LAYER 01 · PHONE

See and direct

Who is online, who is busy, who is stuck. Talk to agents, publish goals, approve risky actions. Installable to your home screen.

LAYER 02 · HUB

Always-on central point

Identity, task routing, conversations and progress events, presence and notifications. The authority for tasks and collaboration records.

LAYER 03 · YOUR COMPUTER

Where the work happens

Repos, terminal, builds and tests, local models. When it is offline, tasks queue — the Hub will not read your workspace for it.

03 — Download

Download for Windows

The desktop app bundles the Hub, the workbench and the execution node — install it, then connect from your phone.

04 — Get started

Three steps

From download to your first roster running.

STEP 01

Install the desktop app

Download and install; it starts the local Hub and workbench. The first launch walks you through creating a space.

STEP 02

Connect your phone

Open /mobile/ on your phone and install it to the home screen. Desktop and mobile share one account.

STEP 03

Publish your first goal

Write what you want done in the console. The roster takes over; progress and evidence come back onto the task for you to accept.
